How to use this tool

1. Pick the module you need: Email Helper or General Templates.

2. Choose the correct Template, then edit the email in English.

3. Update all yellow and red highlighted fields before translating.

4. Pick the first email date for follow-up templates. The tool will auto-fill the +30 day date inside the email.

5. Click Generate Spanish Draft when the English email is ready. The tool uses the approved Spanish template, carries over your edits, and only translates custom English lines. The footer always stays in English.

6. Paste is plain text only, so outside formatting will not carry over.

7. Use Copy email only after the final email is ready.

8. In General Templates, you can also add your own templates and keep them saved on this laptop.
