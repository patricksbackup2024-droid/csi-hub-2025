How to use this tool

1. Select Region first before editing anything.

2. Choose the correct Template, then edit the email in English.

3. Update all yellow and red highlighted fields before translating.

4. Pick the first email date for follow-up templates. The tool will auto-fill the +30 day date inside the email.

5. Click Translate to Spanish when the English email is ready. The footer always stays in English.

6. Paste is plain text only, so outside formatting will not carry over.

7. Use Copy email only after the final email is ready.
